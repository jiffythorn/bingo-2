    How points work
    • Points are score tokens: no cash value, and nothing here can be bought, sold, transferred or cashed out.
    • Every card has a points cost. Those points go into the pot.
    • The pot pays the top three bingos (55 / 25 / 12); whatever is left rolls into the bonus pot, which pays a fast bingo and then resets.
    • Bots pay the card cost like everyone else but never collect their winnings — their share goes to the bonus pot.
    • Your record — games played, games won, points earned — is your leaderboard standing, and the restore code carries it to another device.
